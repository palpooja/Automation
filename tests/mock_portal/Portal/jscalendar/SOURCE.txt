The DHTML Calendar 1.0 by Mihai Bazon / Dynarch.com - the same date-picker component the
URA portal uses on 'Generate Withholding Tax Credit Certificate' (identified from the live
screenshots of 5 Oct 2026: '?', 'x', '<<', '<', 'Today', '>', '>>' buttons, 'wk' column, 'Time:' row).

Used ONLY by the test page wht_dynarch.html, so the tests meet the real component's behaviour:
its buttons and dates react to a mouse button pressed and released on them (mousedown + mouseup),
not to a plain 'click' signal.
